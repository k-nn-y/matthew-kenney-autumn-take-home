# Invented proof

`docs/PRODUCT.md` grants a fabrication licence for proof — plausible inns,
towns, room counts, quotes and figures — on one condition: that every invented
fact is written down, so the line between invented proof and Autumn's real
offer terms is always recoverable. This is that record.

Nothing here is marked on the page itself. A page that labels its own evidence
"illustrative" is not making a case, and the exercise brief asks for a case.

## Not invented — Autumn's actual offer

These are the brief's own terms and are never part of the fabrication licence:

- Google Ads, Google Maps and Google Hotel Ads are the surfaces.
- Autumn covers the ad spend.
- Autumn takes **13% of the bookings it drives**, and nothing in a month where
  it drives none.
- No fixed fees, no agency premium, no long-term contract.
- **10–20% more direct traffic** is the claimed range. It is stated as a range,
  never as a ceiling.
- An online travel agent taking **15–25%** is the industry comparison the page
  argues against.

## Not invented — the photography

The five photographs are stock. The hero is a short generated clip built from
the same scene, used because no real property footage was available to license
for an exercise. `docs/PRODUCT.md` anticipated this: it treats sourced or
generated imagery as expected, and an empty coloured block where a photograph
belongs as a bug.
